Foundation Building

About Us

நாங்கள் யார்?

Vetri Kalaingiyam is a registered government exam coaching centre based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Founded in 2023, we have quickly become one of the most trusted coaching institutions in the region.

Our mission is simple: to make quality government exam coaching accessible to every aspirant regardless of their background. We believe every student who works hard deserves the right guidance to succeed.

We specialize in TNPSC, Bank Exams, Police Exams, SSC, RRB, and TET — providing comprehensive coaching in both Tamil and English medium with expert faculty and proven study methodologies.
